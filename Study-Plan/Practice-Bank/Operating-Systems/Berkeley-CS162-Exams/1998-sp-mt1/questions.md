**Problem #2**<br>
We discussed two mathematical quantities that are reasonable targets for optimization in scheduling:<br>
minimizing ave(f(i)) and minimizing ave(f(i)/s(i)). Please explain what each is, and explain why we would<br>
like to minimize it. (14)

**Problem #3**<br>

**Problem #4**<br>

**Problem #5**<br>
