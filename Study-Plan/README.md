## How it works
- **No dates.** The plan is **184 steps**. Do the lowest unfinished step, tick it, then move to the next. It is written for limited, changing free time (during military service), so the pace is open and only the order matters.
- **Friday** (the one day off) is for the long steps: 2–4h project chunks, virtual contests and flagship coding.
- **No separate review or practice-exam steps** (removed 20 Oct 2026 to save time). The CMU homeworks and projects inside the plan are the checks.
- **Real deadlines are the only exceptions:**
  - Knight-Hennessy (optional), by 6 Oct;
  - booking IELTS, by 15 Oct;
  - the four DAAD steps, by 30 Oct;
  - ETH ESOP, by 30 Nov.

  When one of these dates gets close, do that step first.
