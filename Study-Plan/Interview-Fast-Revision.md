# Interview fast revision: open these before the interview

The [Practice Bank](Practice-Bank/README.md) holds the **hard** material: university exams where the answer takes real work. This page is the opposite: **short question-and-answer pages to read quickly** the day before or the hour before an interview. Each one gives a direct answer under each question.

**Checked:** 20 Oct 2026. Every link below opened. GitHub links were checked through GitHub's raw file server, because github.com blocks automated checks.

## 1. How to revise (the routine)

| When | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **7 days before** | Pick the tracks for that company (§3). For each one, read one long page from §2 and mark every question you can't answer in one sentence. | 1 h per track |
| **Every day until then** | Re-read only the marked questions. Say the answer out loud before you look. | 20–30 min |
| **The day before** | One fast page per track from §2, top to bottom, out loud. Then your STAR stories and the company's own page in [CV/applications/](../Career-Roadmap/CV/applications/README.md). | 1–1.5 h |
| **1 hour before** | Only the questions you marked, plus your 30-second introduction. Nothing new. | 20 min |

**Rule:** if you can't say the answer in two or three sentences, you don't know it yet. Put it back on the list.
