| **B. Remote from Egypt** | Canonical, Collabora, Igalia, Red Hat, or contract platforms ([04](04-Remote-From-Egypt.md)) | Pays in USD or EUR at 3–5× local pay. No visa needed. | **C. Top Egyptian employer → internal transfer** | Microsoft Egypt, Siemens EDA, Synopsys, Valeo ([03](03-Egypt-Companies.md)) | About EGP 0.6–1M+ per year. Big-company brand on the CV. **Internal transfer** avoids the external visa competition. | A transfer usually needs 12+ months of tenure. Transfers are not guaranteed. | **Good.** Siemens EDA and Synopsys like hardware-aware C++ engineers (he has Verilog experience). | 2–4 months to join; 12–24 months to transfer |
| **D. Funded master's abroad** | DAAD, KAUST, Erasmus Mundus, SI, Canada thesis M.Sc., Ireland GOI-IES ([05](05-Masters-and-Scholarships.md)) | A local degree, then an **18–36 month post-study permit** and lower visa salary thresholds. **Bottom line:**
- **A and B are the fastest routes.**
- **C is the safest.**
- **D is insurance.** Its application effort (IELTS, statement of purpose, letters) also strengthens A. That is why the lanes run **in parallel**.

## 5. Application strategy

### 5.1 Three CV versions

Keep one master CV and produce three one-page versions. Each version reorders the same true facts; nothing is invented.

**Done:** the LaTeX sources and PDFs are in [CV/](CV/). Its README says which version to send where and shows the ATS check results.

| Version | Headline | Order of sections | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General SWE** | "Software Engineer — C++ · Rust · Linux · Codeforces Expert" | Skills → Open source → Experience → Projects → Achievements | Big Tech, trading, SaaS |
| **Systems / Rust / open source** | "Systems Software Engineer — Rust · C · Linux · GSoC 2026 (Linux Foundation)" | Open source → Experience → Skills | Canonical, Red Hat, Collabora, JetBrains, Cloudflare |
| **Embedded** | "Embedded Software Engineer — Zephyr · Embedded Linux · Rust" | Experience → Open source (Zephyr first) → Projects | Nordic, NXP, Arm, TomTom, automotive |

### 5.2 Referrals first

Referrals raise response rates far above cold applications.

1. **GSoC mentors** (OpenPrinting, System76). Ask directly for referrals to System76, and for introductions to Canonical (Ubuntu printing) and Red Hat (CUPS).
2. **Zephyr community.** Nordic and NXP engineers review Zephyr PRs, so reach out to them on the Zephyr Discord or LinkedIn.
3. **Egyptian alumni** at Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Siemens EDA and Valeo. Search LinkedIn for "Minia University" plus the company name.
4. **Cold messages** to hiring managers. Keep them to 3 lines: who you are, one proof link, one question.

### 5.3 Volume and timing

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Job applications | **8–12 per week**, spread across tracks 1a, 1b and 1c, plus 2–3 in Egypt or remote |
| Big Tech timing | Graduate and early-career openings peak from **September to December**. Apply **now**. |
| Trading firms (Optiver, IMC) | 2027-start graduate roles are open now. They require a **motivation letter**. |
| Master's | Applications peak from **October to January**. See the roadmap below. |
| Tracking | Update [08 — Application Tracker](08-Application-Tracker.md) the same day you apply. Follow up after 10 business days. |

### 5.4 Offers and negotiation

- Never give a salary number first. Ask for their band.
- Compare offers on **total compensation, net of tax**. Use a net-salary calculator for each country.
- Ask directly about **visa-processing time, relocation budget, temporary housing**, and whether they cover **family visas**.
- One competing offer, even from an Egyptian multinational, changes negotiations.
