## 🌍 Big Tech (multi-country — apply to the EU/UK/Canada offices first)

These companies hire through **one global pipeline**: one application, then choose the office. ## Sponsorship registers — how to verify yourself

| Country | Official source | What to search |
|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 UK | [Register of licensed sponsors: workers (gov.uk)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers) | Download the CSV and search for the company's **legal name** (for example "Bloomberg LP", "Arm Limited"). Check that the route is *Skilled Worker* and the rating is A. |
| 🇳🇱 Netherlands | [IND public register — work & highly skilled migrants](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-regular-labour-and-highly-skilled-migrants) | Company name. Only recognised sponsors can use the HSM route. |
| 🇺🇸 US | [DOL LCA disclosure data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) · [MyVisaJobs](https://www.myvisajobs.com/reports/h1b/) | Employer → job titles and wages actually filed. |
| 🇸🇪 Sweden | [Migrationsverket — certified employers](https://www.migrationsverket.se) | Certified employers get faster work permits. |
| 🇩🇪 Germany | *No public register.* | Ask: "Do you support EU Blue Card applications and relocation?" Big employers have in-house relocation teams. |
| 🇮🇪 Ireland | [DETE employment permits](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/) | Open the *Statistics* section, which lists the permits issued to each company every year. |
