## 🌍 Big Tech (multi-country — apply to the EU/UK/Canada offices first)

These companies hire through **one global pipeline**: one application, then choose the office. ## 🇩🇪 Germany

**Visa:** EU Blue Card. The salary floor is €45,934 for IT and recent graduates. Get the degree listed as **anabin H+** or obtain a ZAB statement first. No job offer yet? The **Opportunity Card** lets you job-hunt on site (see [06](06-Salaries-and-Visas.md#-germany)). Big-tech and scale-up teams work in English; many automotive and Mittelstand teams expect German (B1+).
