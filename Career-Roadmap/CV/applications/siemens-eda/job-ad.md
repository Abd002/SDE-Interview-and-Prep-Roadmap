Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4486077267 · LinkedIn

Siemens EDA is a global technology leader in Electronic Design Automation software. Our software tools enable companies around the world to develop highly innovative electronic products faster and more cost-effectively. Our customers use our tools to push the boundaries of technology and physics to deliver better products in the increasingly complex world of chip, board, and system design.

Position Overview

We are seeking a Software Development Engineer to join our team in Cairo, Egypt.

Key Responsibilities:

- Work on complex software applications using different programming languages and frameworks.
- Contribute to various stages of the software development lifecycle, with a strong focus on software design and programming.
- Communicate daily, both verbally and in writing, with team members and different business units and functions.

Qualifications:

- B.Sc. degree in Computer Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or Computer Science.
- Strong foundation in computer science fundamentals, including object-oriented programming, data structures, and algorithms.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment.
- High degree of independent judgment, accountability, and realistic optimism.
- Relevant work experience is considered an advantage.

Technical Qualifications:

- Hands-on experience with, in-depth knowledge of, and strong proficiency in C++.
- Good knowledge of Python programming.
- Good knowledge of socket programming.
- Good knowledge of multithreaded programming.
