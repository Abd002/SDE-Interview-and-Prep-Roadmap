## 3. How to prepare

- **Fill the gaps in the "nice to have" list cheaply before the interview:**
  - a free IoT fundamentals course (Cisco Networking Academy "Introduction to IoT");
  - AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials (free);
  - a quick read on NB-IoT and LTE-M (why they suit low-power devices).
  Add only what you finish to the CV.
- **Graduate interviews are usually part technical, part behavioural.** Prepare short stories: the EKSON end-to-end system, choosing Zephyr, the 40% asyncio improvement, the IEEE budget, mentoring at ICPC, working with two organizations in Google Summer of Code.
