Job Description:

About The Role

We are looking for motivated software engineers with strong technical foundations who are eager to build a career in embedded and safety-critical software development.

Candidates from Automotive, Telecom, Industrial Automation, SCADA, Embedded Systems, or similar sectors are welcome.

Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain software modules using C/C++.
- Participate in software integration and testing activities.
- Investigate and resolve software defects.
- Contribute to technical documentation.
- Work closely with senior engineers and technical leads.

Essential Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electronics Engineering, or equivalent.
- Good knowledge of C/C++ programming.
- Understanding of embedded systems fundamentals.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Good English communication skills.
- Strong willingness to learn new technologies.

Desirable Requirements

- Experience in embedded software development.
- Knowledge of RTOS environments.
- Familiarity with software testing methodologies.
- Exposure to Automotive, Telecom, Aerospace, Industrial Control, or SCADA systems.
- Basic knowledge of railway systems.

Training

A comprehensive onboarding and technical development program will be provided covering railway applications, ETCS fundamentals, safety-critical development practices, and company engineering processes.
