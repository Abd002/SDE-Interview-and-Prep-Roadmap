Responsibilities:

- Develop software requirements by gathering the project's different stakeholders’ requirements, consolidating them and identifying any conflicting requirements.

- Participate in system and customer requirements analysis for new products. Responsible for system and customer requirements analysis for projects that have a high reuse factor.

- Participate in high level design architecture definition for the new products. Responsible for high level design for the projects that have a high reuse factor.

- Design complicated software component(s) based on defined high-level design.

- Integrate overall software components based on the project’s architecture. This can include traveling to customer sites to ensure smooth delivery.

- Perform technical reviews on software component design, code, and component test, and integrate test strategy and plan.

- Present work progress continuously through active participation of daily & weekly project meetings (Internal and external with other Valeo sites)

- Develop the work breakdown structure and contribute to the estimation of the different project’s activities.

- Contribute effectively in the improvement of standard process, guidelines, checklists, and tools.

- Ensures the efficiency of the validation techniques and standards used by projects

- Perform the responsibilities of the previous technical level as per the projects need.

- Extensive experience in ASPICE-compliant software development, with a strong understanding of quality processes in automotive or embedded systems.Support in performing software integration on the test bench and on the vehicle to ensure the proper functionality.

- Analyze the test reports and issues reported from the customer and lead the action plan to resolve them ensuring customer satisfaction.

- Lead local the warranty issue related to SW and capitalize LLC with PG.

Qualifications:

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.

- 0-1 years of experience in automotive embedded software development.

- Proficiency in C/C++ programming languages for embedded systems in addition to experience with scripting languages like Python.

- Experience with microcontrollers like Renesas, NXP, Aurix.

- Strong understanding of AUTOSAR architecture, including concepts such as service-oriented communication and application lifecycle management is a plus.

- Experience with using Vector tools (CANoe, Davinci, VTest Studio, etc) with different types of automotive communication protocols such as LIN, CAN, and Ethernet.

- Experience with hardware interfacing protocols used in embedded systems including UART, SPI, I2C.

- Experience with software development tools and methodologies, such as version control systems (e.g., Git), application lifecycle management (e.g. Codebeamer), defect management system, and agile development practices.

- Experience with requirements management tools (e.g. Codebeamer, DOORS).

- Strong experience using debugging tools like scopes, logical analyzers, etc.

- Experience with software automatic and manual testing and test report analysis.

- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills, with a strong attention to detail.

- Effective communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.

Other Specifications

- Experience in software development for lighting systems in the automotive industry is highly desirable.

- Knowledge of cybersecurity concepts and best practices for automotive software development is a plus.

- Experience with continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines and automated testing frameworks for automotive software.
