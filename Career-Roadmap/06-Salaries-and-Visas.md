## 1. How to read salary numbers

- **TC (total compensation)** = base salary + bonus + stock, per year. Levels.fyi reports TC. Visa rules usually count **base salary only**.
- **Gross vs net:**
  - Europe and the US quote **gross** pay (before tax).
  - Egypt salaries are often quoted **net per month**.
  - Gulf salaries are tax-free and quoted per month, usually as base + housing + transport allowances.
- **Level matters more than city.** A Google L3 in Amsterdam earns about twice a local bank's junior in the same city (see the table below).
- For about 1 year of experience, use the **entry-level / L3 / SWE I** rows. **Mid** is the stretch target.

## 5. Routes that do not need a job offer first

These routes let you move or apply **before** you have a job:

1. **🇩🇪 Opportunity Card:** move to Germany with about €13k in savings and job-hunt for 12 months.
2. **🇨🇦 Express Entry:** apply for PR directly. Needs IELTS General (CLB 7+) and a WES evaluation. Being under 30 and having CS experience help the score.
3. **🇬🇧 Global Talent:** needs a strong evidence portfolio. A realistic target once he has 1–2 more years of upstream work.
4. **Master's → post-study permit:**
   - 🇩🇪 18 months;
   - 🇳🇱 12 months (orientation year);
   - 🇬🇧 18–24 months;
   - 🇮🇪 12–24 months (Stamp 1G);
   - 🇨🇦 up to 3 years (PGWP).

   See [05](05-Masters-and-Scholarships.md).
