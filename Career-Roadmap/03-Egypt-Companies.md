# 03 — Egypt-Based Companies

> **Why this file matters:** a strong Egyptian job is not a detour. It can help in three ways:
> 1. **Salary now.** Microsoft Egypt pays about EGP 1M per year.
> 2. **Experience** in backend and system design, which fills the gaps in [01](01-Profile-Analysis.md).
> 3. **An internal transfer abroad** after about 12 months, without competing in the external visa market.
>
> **Verified on:** 2026-09-29, via web search (`S`). Pages were not opened. Rows marked **"to confirm"** mean the company is known in Egypt, but its local **engineering** openings were not confirmed in the search results.
> Visa columns are left out because they don't apply in Egypt. The "Path abroad" column replaces them.
> Careers links for smaller companies point to their homepages, taken from general knowledge. They were **not opened**, so check them.

**41 companies.** Local salary context from the [cvpromaker.com Egypt survey](https://www.cvpromaker.com/salaries/egypt/software-engineer) (July 2026, 2,590 responses):
- **Junior:** EGP 10–22k/month net.
- **Mid-level:** EGP 18–42k/month net, rising to EGP 28–55k at top companies.
- Multinational R&D centres pay **far more**, for example Microsoft at about EGP 1.0M/year TC.
