## 4. Contracts, payments and tax (Egypt)

| Topic | What to know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee vs contractor** | Most foreign companies without an Egyptian entity either pay you as a **contractor** or employ you through an **employer of record** (Deel, Remote.com). An employer of record gives you a local contract and social insurance; a contractor setup pays more gross but you handle tax yourself. | [remotivated.com](https://remotivated.com/best-remote-companies/benefits/work-from-anywhere) |

## 5. How to get hired remotely with his profile

1. **Lead with public code.** Put the GSoC report, the `cosmic-printers` repo and the merged PR links in the first three lines of the CV.
2. **Referrals from mentors.** Ask the OpenPrinting and System76 mentors to introduce you to Canonical (Ubuntu printing), Red Hat (CUPS packaging), System76 and Collabora.
3. **Write asynchronously.** Remote-first companies such as Canonical test written communication. Prepare a well-written project write-up, and reuse the GSoC final report.
4. **Time zone.** Egypt (UTC+2/+3) overlaps fully with European working hours, which is an advantage for EU-based remote teams.
5. **Keep contributing** to one upstream project that a target company maintains, for example CUPS/libcups (Red Hat, Canonical), Mesa or GStreamer (Collabora, Igalia), or Zephyr (Nordic, NXP).
